# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Dr. Ferdous Salehin Official Digital Platform

**Product Type:** Author, Dawah, Knowledge, Podcast, Audiobook and Publication Platform
**Primary Language:** Bangla
**Secondary Language Support:** English where required
**Primary Audience:** Bangladesh and Bangla-speaking audiences worldwide
**Prepared by:** FrameCipher
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Development Specification

---

# 1. Product Overview

The proposed platform will be the official digital home of **Dr. Ferdous Salehin**, bringing his books, Islamic reflections, podcast, audiobook, lectures, videos, articles, events, and future intellectual work into a single structured digital ecosystem.

The website should not function merely as a promotional website for the book **“সুরা আল-ফাতিহা: সাতটি আয়াত”**.

The product should instead establish a scalable digital platform where visitors can:

* Discover Dr. Ferdous Salehin and his work
* Learn about his books
* Read selected Islamic reflections
* Study content related to Surah Al-Fatihah
* Listen to podcast episodes
* Listen to audiobooks directly on the website
* Watch lectures and videos
* Read articles
* Order physical books
* Discover upcoming events
* Submit speaking invitations
* Download selected dawah resources
* Subscribe for future updates
* Share beneficial content with others

The first book will serve as one of the major pillars of the platform, while the architecture must support future books, podcast series, audiobooks, lectures, courses, and other initiatives.

---

# 2. Product Vision

The long-term vision is to create a permanent and credible digital platform for Dr. Ferdous Salehin's intellectual and dawah work.

Social media should serve primarily as a distribution channel. The website should become the permanent destination where the complete body of work is organised, searchable, accessible, and preserved.

The platform should gradually become a digital library containing:

* Books
* Audiobooks
* Podcasts
* Islamic reflections
* Qur'anic discussions
* Lectures
* Articles
* Video content
* Events
* Reader experiences
* Downloadable resources
* Future publications

The experience should feel more like an Islamic knowledge and intellectual platform than a conventional personal website.

---

# 3. Product Objectives

The primary objectives are:

### 3.1 Establish an Official Digital Identity

Create one authoritative online destination representing Dr. Ferdous Salehin, his biography, publications, work, media appearances, and future initiatives.

### 3.2 Extend the Reach of the Book

Allow visitors who discover the author or book through Facebook, YouTube, Google, podcasts, events, or personal recommendations to continue learning through the website.

### 3.3 Create a Permanent Dawah Platform

Provide useful Islamic content even for visitors who have not purchased the physical book.

### 3.4 Build a Podcast Destination

Create a dedicated podcast environment where episodes remain organised and accessible independently of YouTube, Facebook, Spotify, or other external platforms.

### 3.5 Provide an Integrated Audiobook Experience

Allow visitors to listen to audiobook chapters directly from the website through a dedicated player.

### 3.6 Centralise Content

Bring books, articles, podcasts, videos, events, and resources under a common content architecture.

### 3.7 Build Direct Audience Relationships

Create mechanisms for newsletter subscription, inquiries, speaking invitations, reader feedback, book orders, and future communication.

### 3.8 Prepare for Future Expansion

The underlying structure should support additional books, audiobook series, podcasts, Islamic resources, courses, and other digital initiatives without requiring a complete website rebuild.

Required functionality:

* Play
* Pause
* Seek
* Progress bar

* Total duration
* Volume control
* Playback speed
* Previous chapter
* Next chapter

Recommended playback speeds:

* 0.75×
* 1×
* 1.25×
* 1.5×
* 2×

## 12.5 Persistent Audio Playback

If technically feasible within implementation, audio should continue while the user navigates between website pages.

For example:

A visitor starts Chapter 3, opens an article, and the audio continues playing.

This significantly improves audiobook usability.

## 12.6 Resume Listening

Where browser storage permits, the website should remember:

* Last audiobook
* Last chapter
* Approximate playback position

User account creation is not required for this feature.

Browser local storage may be used.

# 39. Error States

The website must provide clear messages for common errors.

## Empty Search

**আপনার অনুসন্ধানের সঙ্গে মিল পাওয়া যায়নি। অন্য কোনো শব্দ দিয়ে খুঁজে দেখুন।**

## Failed Form Submission

**তথ্য পাঠানো সম্ভব হয়নি। অনুগ্রহ করে আবার চেষ্টা করুন।**

## Missing Page

Custom 404 page:

**এই পৃষ্ঠাটি পাওয়া যায়নি।**

Provide links to:

* Home
* Books
* Podcast
* Reflections

## Missing Audio

If an audiobook chapter is unavailable:

**এই অডিওটি বর্তমানে পাওয়া যাচ্ছে না।**

The page itself should remain usable.

# 47. Final Product Definition

The final website should be understood as:

> **The official digital platform of Dr. Ferdous Salehin, bringing together his books, Surah Al-Fatihah reflections, podcast, audiobook, articles, videos, lectures, events, and future dawah initiatives within one permanent digital ecosystem.**

The current book should provide the initial foundation, but the product should never be architected around only one book.

The core principle is:

> **The book introduces the message. The digital platform allows that message to be read, heard, watched, explored, shared, and preserved over time.**

This PRD deliberately excludes the **admin dashboard**, as requested.
